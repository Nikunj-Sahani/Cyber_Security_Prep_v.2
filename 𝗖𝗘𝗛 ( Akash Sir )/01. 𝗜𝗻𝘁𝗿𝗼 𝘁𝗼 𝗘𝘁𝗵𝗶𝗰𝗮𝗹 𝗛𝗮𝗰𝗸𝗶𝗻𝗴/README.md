### What is Cyber Security ?
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks and data from digital attacks & unauthorized acces.

## Element of Cybersecurity 
Element of CyberSecurity is explained in three word **Confidentiality , Integrity , Availability** which is popularly known as **CIA triad.**

### 🔐 CIA Traid
The CIA Triad helps keep information private, correct, and ready to use.

1. **Confidentiality:-** Keeping Information Secret
2. **Integrity:-** Keeping Information Correct
3. **Availability :-** Making Information Easy to Get

- [X] 👉 **This is also Part of CIA Traid.**

4. **Authentication :-** Proving Who You Are
5. **Authorization :-** Giving the Right Permissions
6. **Non-Repudiation :-** Proof of Actions)
7. **Accountability :-** Taking Responsibility
8. **Risk Management :-** Thinking Ahead to Stay Safe

- **These three things are key for any information security system.**

 > - **Disclosure –** When an authorized party gets access to your information.
 > - **Alteration –** When data is modified or changed.
 > - **Destruction –** When data, systems or applications are destroyed or rendered inaccessible.

## 🧱 Cyber Kill Chain
Cyber Kill Chain is a model that identifies the steps followed by attackers to carry out a cyber attack.

### 📌🧱 7 Stages of Cyber Kill Chain

- **1️⃣ Reconnaissance**
   > - Attacker collects information about the target
   > - Emails, IPs, domains, employees

- **2️⃣ Weaponization**
   > - Creates malicious payload
   > - Malware + exploit combined

- **3️⃣ Delivery**
   > - Sends malware to victim
   > - Email attachment, USB, website

- **4️⃣ Exploitation**
   > - Exploit runs on victim system
   > - Vulnerability is triggered

- **5️⃣ Installation**
   > - Malware installed on system
   > - Backdoor or trojan created

- **6️⃣ Command & Control (C2)**
   > - Attacker communicates with infected system
   > - Remote access established

- **7️⃣ Actions on Objectives**
   > - Data theft
   > - Privilege escalation
   > - System damage
